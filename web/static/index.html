Import daily climate, adjust soils, and compare a climate sensitivity scenario. Calculations run in a Web Worker on your device. Your files stay in this browser.

Legacy research calculator. This is a TypeScript adaptation of the 2019 forestDroughtTool model. It does not recreate the newer published forDRAT estimates. Its output concerns the model’s water balance; it is not a planting recommendation.

1. Climate and calculation

Columns: date,tmn,tmx,ppt. Dates use YYYY-MM-DD; temperatures use °C and precipitation mm/day. Supply complete, continuous calendar years in order. Missing days and values are rejected.

2. Soil parameters

Defaults are decoded from the original R package. All five regimes run together; they do not identify the soil at your site. Negative lateral drainage represents the legacy model’s water subsidy.

Method and run notes

    A traceable browser port

    The daily recurrence follows the pinned R kernel, including its final-47-record snow initialization, zero-PET ratio of one, and lateral-flow rules. Chronological input validation and explicit summary weighting are deliberate browser adaptations. The published forDRAT explorer remains a separate workflow.
